About Velvet Golf

Velvet Golf is mini-golf with one course for each particle of the Standard Model, plus the hypothetical graviton: 18 courses in all. Each course has three levels of three holes, and its twist comes from its particle.

Courses so far

  • Electron (free), Circuit City: charged posts. Your ball is negative, so red + posts pull it and blue − posts push it away.
  • Up quark, Summit Trail: every hole climbs, and a putt that is too soft rolls back down.
  • Down quark, The Mine: every hole descends, and mine carts cross on rails.
  • Photon, Night Funfair: mirrors. The ball leaves a mirror at the angle it arrived.

More courses are on the way. Each level gives a bronze, silver or gold star.

Questions and mulligans

Answer questions from your course to win mulligans, which take back a putt. The course you choose in Velvet Blaster sets the questions.

What you need

A computer with a mouse. The Electron course is free; the others open with Velvet Coins, earned from mini-lessons and the Revision Arcade on aistudymethod.com.
