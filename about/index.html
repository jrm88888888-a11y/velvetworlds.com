About Velvet Worlds

Velvet Worlds is a set of browser games for secondary-school science students, made as a reward for studying. Students earn Velvet Coins from mini-lessons and the Revision Arcade on aistudymethod.com, then spend them here on new worlds, courses and features.

The games

  • Velvet Blaster: every element of the periodic table is a 3D world to explore, with questions from the student’s own course.
  • Velvet Golf: mini-golf with one course for each particle of the Standard Model.
  • Velvet Racing: coming soon, because students asked for it.

How coins work

  • Coins are earned on aistudymethod.com, by finishing mini-lessons and playing the Revision Arcade.
  • Here they open elements in Velvet Blaster (an element costs its atomic number in coins), golf courses, and items in each game’s shop.
  • The first Velvet Blaster world and the Electron golf course are free.

What the games are like

  • There is no blood or gore. In Velvet Blaster the enemies are robots and monsters that pop when they are hit.
  • There is no chat or messaging between players.
  • The games need a computer: Velvet Blaster uses a keyboard and mouse, Velvet Golf a mouse. They are made for home: school networks often block game sites, so the lessons on aistudymethod.com are the part made for school.

Accounts and data

  • Accounts are optional and are shared with aistudymethod.com. An account is a username, a password and a recovery key. No name, email address or date of birth is asked for.
  • An account keeps coins, collected elements and game progress across devices. Without one, students can try the free world and the free course.
  • The site uses Plausible Analytics, which sets no cookies and collects no personal data. Game files are delivered by Bunny.net.
  • The full details are in the privacy policy.
