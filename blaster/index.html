About Velvet Blaster

Velvet Blaster is a 3D adventure game built on the periodic table. Every element is a world, from a castle and a coral reef to a frozen tundra and a crystal cave. Collect an element with Velvet Coins and its world opens.

How it works

  1. Choose your course. The questions change with it; the worlds stay the same.
  2. Explore the world and find the pieces hidden in it. Answer questions to recharge your blaster, and pop the robots and monsters that get in the way.
  3. Take the pieces back to the base for the level’s final task. Each level you finish puts a star over the element: bronze, silver or gold.
  4. Protons, neutrons and electrons are hidden in every world. Build them into atoms, then bond the atoms into compounds in the Forge to make new blasters.

Courses

KS3 science (chemistry topics so far). GCSE biology, chemistry and physics, Foundation and Higher (the questions follow the AQA specifications). A-level biology, chemistry and physics for AQA, OCR A, Edexcel, Eduqas and CCEA. IB Diploma biology, chemistry and physics at SL and HL. AP Biology, AP Chemistry, AP Physics 1, AP Physics 2 and AP Physics C.

What you need

A computer with a keyboard and a mouse. On a phone or tablet you can collect elements, but the worlds need a computer. The first world is free. The others open with Velvet Coins, earned from mini-lessons and the Revision Arcade on aistudymethod.com.
